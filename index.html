Privacy Policy for Card Mate

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Introduction

Card Mate ("the App", "we", "us") is a card game score tracking application for Marriage and Call Break. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Do Not Collect

Card Mate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information.
  • We do not require user accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not access your device's contacts, calendar, or files outside of what you explicitly select.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Card Mate stores the following information only on your device, using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server:

  • Player names you enter (for game tracking).
  • Profile pictures you select from your device's gallery or camera.
  • Game scores, bids, and round history for Marriage and Call Break games.
  • App preferences (such as points-per-rupee setting).

You can delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the App, or by using the "New Game" reset feature within the App.

Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a profile picture for a player. You can deny this permission and still use the App.
  • Storage / Photos — only when you choose to select a profile picture from your gallery. You can deny this permission and still use the App.

Third-Party Services

Card Mate does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud storage providers.

Children's Privacy

Card Mate does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

All data stored by the App resides in your device's private application storage, protected by your device's operating system. We have no access to this data.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
